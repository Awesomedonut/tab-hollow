# Native farming surface adaptation — 2026-09-16

The prepared beds now use cropped native Ridgeside spirit-realm dirt edges and texture. Rounded outer lips, four independently joined quadrants and small concave diagonal notches replace the old flat procedural rectangle. Wet/dry palettes share geometry so watering a single tile does not split the bed. This is repurposed ground artwork, not a claim to reproduce Stardew's hoe-dirt atlas.

Turnips now use four 16×32 PNG cells adapted from Nina's RePixel radish, already used in the inventory as a cream/purple turnip. Seedling, two-leaf growing, fuller leafy and mature bulb silhouettes are distinct. Native leaf clusters are rearranged on integer pixels; no smoothing or rescaling is baked into assets. **The growth sequence is a Clover Hollow adaptation, not a native growth sheet authored by Nina.** The September 16 follow-up replaces the mature inventory item with a small emerging bulb shoulder beneath a rooted crown. All stages now connect native leaf fans to a shaded central petiole.
