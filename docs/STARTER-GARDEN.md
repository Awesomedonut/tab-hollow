# The starter garden

A new farm begins with six real turnip plants in the southeast corner of the interactive garden: `(22,16)`, `(23,16)`, `(22,17)`, `(23,17)`, `(22,18)`, and `(23,18)`.

Their soil is tilled and watered, and their initial growth is 1 of 3. After the first night they reach stage 2; water them the next day and sleep again to harvest on day 3. They use the same watering, growth, harvest, shipping, and save rules as player-planted turnips. Their first watering is already done.

The player still receives 12 turnip seeds, 80g, and full energy. The six starter plants are an additional welcome gift and can count toward the first 12-turnip shipment quest. They make the opening farm visibly cultivated while leaving the remaining 64 plots for the player.

Existing saved farms are restored exactly as saved. Loading never adds starter crops or regrows harvested ones. Choosing New Farm starts with the six plants again.
