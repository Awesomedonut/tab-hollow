# Some Building Edits — building artwork

The cottage, market, and well use artwork by **MourningStar / TheMourningStar** from [Some Building Edits](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/3113).

Author permission, updated **19 December 2023** on that page:

> So if its still of use, and anyone is so inclined to, feel free to use anything from this mod however you see fit. Mod it more, edit it share it, doesn't bother me at all.

This newer explicit author statement supersedes the older page's restrictive modification/asset checkboxes. The page also explicitly permits conversion with creator credit. The mod credits elements of vanilla Stardew Valley and inspiration from strawberrymilk's Witchy House. Those underlying contributions are not claimed as original Clover Hollow artwork, public domain, or covered by our source-code license.

## Source and adaptation

Source: the author's [full building preview image](https://staticdelivery.nexusmods.com/mods/1303/images/3113/3113-1544389007-1268692174.png), 694 × 754 pixels.

- Cottage: rectangle `[32,114,144,144]`.
- Market: rectangle `[32,259,144,144]`.
- Well: rectangle `[215,466,50,79]`.
- The exterior connected white background is removed; interior white siding is retained.
- No geometry, paint, or palette is synthesized. These are preview-derived crops, **not pristine native sprite sheets**. The author's preview already has some resampling softness. They are drawn at one source pixel per world pixel without fractional scaling.
- `public/assets/mourningstar/extract.py` reproduces the crops from the cited image.

The original downloadable archive requires an authenticated Nexus or Chucklefish Forums session. We used the openly accessible author preview and did not bypass the download login.
