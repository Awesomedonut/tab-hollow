NOTES FROM THE VALLEY

Your field guide

Good things take a little tending.

01

Make room to grow

Walk to the garden below the cottage. Twelve turnips at different stages are already growing, and twelve empty plots are prepared. You have six seeds to plant. Plant there, or use the hoe to prepare more earth inside the corner stakes.

02

A little water, every day

Water your crops, then visit the cottage door to sleep. Turnips ripen after three watered nights.

03

Bring in the harvest

Use the harvest tool on ripe turnips. Take them to the shipping box beside your cottage to earn 25g each.

04

Keep the good growing

Buy 6 seeds for 30g at the village shop. Ship 12 turnips and earn 300g to complete your first chapter.
