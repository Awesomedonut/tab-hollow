The artists behind the valley

Tab Hollow combines community artwork with its own farm layout, characters, crops and gameplay. Thank you to these artists.

Native Ridgeside Village art

The playable Ian character, warm farmhouse, seed shop, well, shipping box, ground and water tiles, fences, barrels, pier boards, chickens and wooden signs come from Ridgeside Village, by Rafseazz and the Ridgeside Village graphics contributors: Aubs, Avislone, Bodyartbrandon, Brockens, D1scoball, Emily, Foggywizard, KediDili, Keksgesicht, Maistress, Miriam Lettuce, PandorasBox, Paperpoppy, Pillow, PrismaFrost, Stardew People, Thatdude, Wakkety Sax and XxHarvzBackxX.

Artwork outside the mod's SMAPI Component is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Our cropped adaptations retain that license. Character: native walking and side-working crops, project-adapted front/back hoe and four-direction watering body poses, and original tool overlays. Adapted poses are not upstream-authored farming frames. Buildings: native transparent crops; the well's rectangular translucent backing is removed. Building and walking-character crops keep native opaque pixels; adapted action poses rearrange body pieces and add articulated arms. Chickens and signs are native crops with normalized frame padding, directional mirroring and original sign text; prop source details. Terrain preserves native geometry with a documented warm earth palette adaptation. Full upstream credits · Source license statement · License · Character provenance · Building provenance.

Mod artwork

  • Simple Resources by Azrashar / itsanette / dugongkebalik: native mod rocks, logs, weeds and stumps, used under published noncommercial reuse/conversion permissions with credit. Source and permissions.
  • Simple Foliage by Azrashar / itsanette (dugongkebalik): maple, pine, mahogany, cherry, bushes and tufts. Native transparent crops, used under the author's published reuse and other-game conversion permissions with credit. Sources and crop details.
  • Some Building Edits by MourningStar / TheMourningStar: historical cottage, market and well artwork, superseded by native Ridgeside sprites. Cropped from the author's public building preview with its exterior white background removed. Used under the author's December 2023 permission to use, edit and share the mod. The mod credits vanilla Stardew Valley elements and inspiration from strawberrymilk's Witchy House. Source and adaptation.
  • Stardew Valley Item RePixel by Nina: harvested turnip icon, recolored from the radish. The author permits use beyond the game. Permission and credit.
